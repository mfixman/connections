\begin{block}{A Failed Tableau Can Still \\ Contribute to the Proof}
	\large
	Connection provers \cite{leancop} search for a closed tableau, and normally discard a failed attempt.

	SATCoP \cite{rawson2021eliminating} also grounds each tableau clause and adds it to a SAT solver.
	If these clauses become unsatisfiable the problem is refuted, even with no closed tableau.

	Clauses from failed attempts stay in the solver, and its model suggests where to search next.
\end{block}
